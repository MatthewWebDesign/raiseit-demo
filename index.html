Ideas & help

Frequently asked questions

Can't see your question? Ask our assistant using the chat bubble.

Is it safe for students?

Yes. Student avatars replace photos, page links are randomised, there's no public search, first names only are recommended, and classes aren't publicly identified.

Can we run a colour run without an event company?

Yes. Most schools run their own, with our guides and support.

We don't have many volunteers. What works best?

Online events cut down admin, because donations and sponsorships are collected online.

Which fundraisers raise the most?

Big participation events like colour runs, fun runs and a-thons, because they involve the whole school community and online sponsorship.

Do we have to spend money on prizes?

No. You set your own prize levels and budget, using your own prizes, sponsor prizes or the spot prize shop.
